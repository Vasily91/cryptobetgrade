Cookies

A short, honest page: one strictly-necessary cookie, two local preferences, and nothing else. Here's exactly what happens when you load a page.

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Current status: one strictly-necessary cookie, two local preferences — no analytics, no advertising. Logging in sets a single cookie that keeps you signed in. Dismissing the notice at the bottom of the page, and choosing dark or light mode, each save that choice in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. None of this is used for tracking, analytics, or advertising, and no third-party ad or analytics cookie is set by cryptobetgrade.com.

1. What a "cookie" is, briefly

A cookie is a small piece of data a website asks your browser to store and send back on later visits — usually to remember who you are, a preference, or to track behaviour across visits for analytics or advertising. This Site asks your browser to store the minimum needed to keep you logged in, and nothing for tracking or advertising.

Loading this Site's pages does trigger a small number of automatic requests to services we don't control, even though none of them set a cookie in your browser via this Site:

These are standard, publicly documented services used purely to render the page correctly (the site's font, each operator's logo, and the country-acceptance check on profile pages) — not for tracking by us. What Google, DuckDuckGo, or ipwho.is themselves do with that request is governed by their own privacy policies, not ours. The country-acceptance check is informational only — it's based on our own research into each operator's stated restrictions, which may be incomplete or outdated, and is never a substitute for checking the operator's own terms before depositing.

4. The notice you see at the bottom of the page

The small banner shown on this Site is a transparency notice, not a legally-required consent gate — under GDPR/ePrivacy rules, consent is only required before setting non-essential cookies, and this Site sets none (the one cookie it does set is strictly necessary and exempt, see Section 2). Clicking "Got it" saves that choice in your browser's localStorage so it doesn't reappear on later visits.

5. If this changes

The first time this Site adds analytics, an ad, or anything that needs a non-essential cookie, this page and the table above get updated with the actual cookie name, purpose, and duration — and the bottom banner becomes a real accept/decline control rather than an acknowledgement.
