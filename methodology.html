The 5 scoring categories

Each operator is scored across 5 categories to calculate the final CryptoBetGrade.

✓

1. Licence & regulation

Licence strength, regulator quality and enforcement history.

25%
↗

2. Payout reliability

Withdrawal speed, limits and history of delayed or denied payouts.

25%
⚑

3. Complaint record

Complaint severity, disputed value and how cases are resolved.

25%
✓

4. KYC practice

Whether verification is proportionate, clear and fairly applied.

12.5%
⚖

5. T&C fairness

One-sided clauses, voiding rules, arbitrage restrictions and withdrawal terms.

12.5%
☆ Final score is weighted and updated when new evidence or complaint outcomes appear.
