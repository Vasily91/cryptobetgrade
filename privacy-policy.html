Privacy Policy

How CryptoBetGrade handles personal data, third-party requests and your privacy rights.

Last updated: 2026-08-24

1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CryptoBetGrade handles personal data when you visit cryptobetgrade.com (the “Site”), contact us, submit information to us, or otherwise interact with the Site.

CryptoBetGrade is an independent informational website focused on crypto sportsbooks and related operator information. CryptoBetGrade does not operate a sportsbook or casino and does not process gambling deposits or withdrawals.

2. Data we may receive

Information you provide directly. If you contact us by email, submit a dispute, report an issue, suggest an operator, or otherwise send information to CryptoBetGrade, we may receive the information you choose to provide. This may include your name or online handle, email address, the content of your message, supporting documents, screenshots, transaction references, and other information relevant to your submission.

Technical information. When you access the Site, your browser necessarily communicates technical information such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested page, and request time to the services needed to deliver the Site and its resources. CryptoBetGrade does not use this information to identify visitors unless necessary for security, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.

3. Third-party resources loaded by the Site

The Site currently uses certain externally hosted resources. When your browser requests those resources, the relevant provider may receive technical information including your IP address:

  • Google Fonts — used to load the Site's typeface.
  • Google favicon services and DuckDuckGo icon services — may be used to display operator website icons or logos.

These providers process requests under their own privacy terms. See our Cookies page for additional information about browser storage and tracking technologies used by the Site.

4. Why we process personal data

Where the GDPR applies, we process personal data only where there is an appropriate legal basis. Depending on the context, this may include:

  • Legitimate interests — operating, securing and improving the Site; responding to legitimate enquiries; maintaining accurate operator and complaint records; and preventing abuse or fraud.
  • Consent — where you voluntarily provide information for a purpose that requires consent, or where consent is required for a particular technology or processing activity.
  • Legal obligations — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law, regulatory requirements, court orders or other binding legal obligations.

5. Complaint and dispute submissions

If you submit a complaint, dispute, correction request or supporting evidence, please provide only information necessary for us to assess the matter. Do not send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, full payment-card details, or other credentials.

Information supplied in connection with a complaint or dispute may be reviewed against publicly available sources and, where appropriate, may be shared with the relevant operator or service provider for the purpose of checking or responding to the claim. We may publish a summary of a complaint or its outcome where this is appropriate for the Site, but we aim to avoid publishing unnecessary personal information.

6. What we do not do

CryptoBetGrade does not process gambling deposits or withdrawals and does not receive your sportsbook account credentials. Deposits, withdrawals, KYC checks and gambling activity performed on an operator's website are handled by that operator and are subject to its own privacy policy and terms.

We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

7. Service providers and international transfers

We may use hosting, email, security, analytics or other technical service providers to operate CryptoBetGrade. Those providers may process limited personal data on our behalf or receive technical request data as part of providing their services.

If personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we will use an applicable transfer mechanism where required, such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to respond to enquiries or disputes, maintain the integrity of published complaint records, establish or defend legal claims, comply with legal obligations, and protect the Site from abuse.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it is held. Where information is no longer required, it will be deleted or anonymised where reasonably practicable.

9. Your rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you may have rights including access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise a privacy right, contact privacy@cryptobetgrade.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority. In Lithuania, this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija).

10. Cookies and similar technologies

Information about cookies, local storage and similar technologies used by CryptoBetGrade is provided on our dedicated Cookies page.

11. Children

CryptoBetGrade is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children.

12. External websites

The Site contains links to third-party websites, including sportsbook and casino operators. CryptoBetGrade is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of those third-party websites. Review the relevant provider's privacy information before providing personal data to it.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Site, our data practices, or applicable legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to privacy@cryptobetgrade.com.
