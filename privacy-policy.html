Privacy Policy

What CryptoBetGrade collects today, what it doesn't, and what a live version of this product would need before launch.

Last updated: 2026-08-24

Draft — not legal advice. This is a starting template written for the current, static version of this site, not a finished, lawyer-reviewed policy. Before this product collects any real personal data, have a qualified lawyer (ideally one familiar with GDPR and gambling-adjacent advertising rules) review and finalise it — every [bracketed] field below needs a real value first.

1. Where this stands today

CryptoBetGrade, as it exists right now, is a static set of pages with no server, no database, and no real user-account system. That has a direct effect on this policy: there is currently no working mechanism on this site that collects personal data from an ordinary visitor. The "Log in" / "Sign up" links open a placeholder explaining that player accounts don't exist yet, and the admin area is a client-side password gate for the site's own team, not a real authentication system. The sections below describe what applies once a real backend exists — most of it is not yet in effect.

2. Who this will apply to

This policy covers cryptobetgrade.com ("the Site"), an informational site that reviews and tracks publicly available information about crypto sportsbook and casino operators. It does not cover the operators listed on the Site — each has its own privacy practices, linked from their profile page where available.

3. What we collect

Visitors browsing the Site. No analytics tool is currently installed, so no browsing data is collected or stored by us today. If/when an analytics tool is added (e.g. a privacy-focused, cookie-free option like Plausible, or Google Analytics), this section will name it, describe what it collects (typically: approximate location from IP, device/browser type, pages visited, referring site), and state the legal basis (legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or consent where required).

Anyone submitting a complaint or a "suggest a sportsbook" form. These forms exist in the design but aren't wired up to actually send or store anything yet. Once live, submitted data (name or handle, email, and the content submitted) would be collected on the basis of consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Admin users. The current admin login is a single shared password checked entirely in your browser — no login attempt, email, or credential is transmitted anywhere or stored server-side. A real version would use a proper auth provider with individual accounts; this section will be rewritten once that exists.

4. What we don't collect

We don't process payments on this Site — any deposit happens on the operator's own site, entirely outside our systems. We don't require account creation to browse or search. We don't use browser local storage to remember anything about you.

5. Requests your browser makes automatically

Even with no backend of our own, loading this Site's pages makes a small number of automatic requests to third parties, each of which may see your IP address as part of that request:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — loads the site's typeface.
  • Google's favicon service & DuckDuckGo's icon service — used to fetch each crypto sportsbook's logo on its profile and on the homepage's top-5 cards, with one as a fallback if the other doesn't have it.

See Cookies for how this relates to cookies specifically.

7. Retention

Not applicable today — nothing is stored. Once live: visitor analytics data would be kept for [X months]; submitted complaints retained as long as relevant to the operator's public profile or until deletion is requested; admin account data for the duration of the person's role plus [X].

8. Your rights (EU/EEA visitors, GDPR)

Once this Site processes personal data, you'll be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and request a portable copy. Contact privacy@cryptobetgrade.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in Lithuania, that's the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija).

9. Cookies

See the dedicated Cookies page for the current, accurate list — short version: this Site does not set any cookies of its own today.

10. Children

The Site is not directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top when this changes materially — most notably, the first time this Site actually starts collecting real data.
