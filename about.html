ABOUT

Sportsbook reviews with the marketing stripped out

CryptoBetGrade is an independent research project that scores crypto sportsbooks from public complaint records, published terms, and on-chain deposit data — not from what the operators say about themselves.

Most "best crypto sportsbook" lists are written by people paid per signup, so the ranking tends to follow the commission, not the player's actual experience. CryptoBetGrade started from a simple annoyance with that: there was nowhere to check what actually happens when a payout gets delayed or a bonus term gets disputed, only pages that repeat the same "licensed, secure, instant withdrawals" copy for every operator.

So instead of editorial opinions, every Trust Score here is computed from a fixed set of inputs — licensing status, published terms, tracked complaint outcomes, and (where an operator's flows are visible on-chain) real deposit data — using the same weighting for every operator. The full breakdown of how that works is on the methodology page.

What we optimize for

Independence over reach

Four rules the site is built around, not just stated in a footer.

✓

Same formula for every operator

Trust Scores come from a fixed formula applied the same way to every operator — nobody gets a better score because a writer likes them more. The formula's own weighting is CryptoBetGrade's editorial judgment, documented on the How Scoring Works page.

🔒

No sportsbook can buy a score

Affiliate deals change nothing about how an operator is scored. A sportsbook with no affiliate relationship at all is scored exactly the same way.

⏱

Independent complaint records

Reported issues and their outcomes stay visible on an operator's profile — they don't get quietly removed because an operator would prefer a cleaner page.

⛓

On-chain, not self-reported

Where an operator's deposit flows are visible on public blockchain analytics, we use that instead of the volume numbers an operator publishes about itself.

How this stays free

Affiliate links, disclosed

CryptoBetGrade doesn't charge visitors and doesn't sell placement in the Trust Score. Some operator profiles include an affiliate link — clearly marked as such on that profile — and if you sign up through one, we may earn a referral commission at no extra cost to you.

That relationship never flows back into scoring. The formula runs before any affiliate deal is considered, and an operator with no affiliate link is never penalized for it. If we ever change that, this page and the methodology page get updated first.

Honesty check

Where this stands today

This is an independent, unofficial review site, not a fully built product yet. Every complaint listed is drawn from a real public source — AskGamblers, Casino.Guru, the BBB, Trustpilot, or a named forum/subreddit — and individually cited; click through on any complaint to check it yourself. Trust Scores are this site's own editorial judgment, weighted by the criteria on the How scoring works page — not a certified or independently audited rating.

  • Player accounts use real, passwordless login — an email link, no password to remember or store. Logged-in visitors can file a complaint about an operator directly from its page; it goes through CryptoBetGrade's review before it's shown publicly.
  • "Suggest a sportsbook" and "Contact / submit a dispute" both work today — they open a pre-filled email instead of a form.
  • If you run one of the operators listed and believe a specific complaint is inaccurate or missing context, use the dispute contact — corrections get reflected here.

Site accuracy hasn't gone through a separate legal review. If something here looks wrong, tell us — see the dispute contact above.
